Norton Rose Fulbright has a sector led approach. Which one of our sectors do you think has the most potential for growth over the next 12 months, and why, and what opportunities do you see for the firm to maximise this potential? (300 words) The sector with the most potential for growth over the next year is technology, largely due to the technological, commercial and regulatory developments in the field of AI. In September, Microsoft, Salesforce and Workday announced plans to develop AI agents which can act on behalf of users (where current AI assistants only suggest), extending commercial applications of AI tools. As AI tools become workplace staples, more businesses will need support navigating this technology. AI startups continue to emerge, with newcomer Sakana AI valued at $1.5bn this month and Microsoft entering into partnership with the then 10-month-old Mistral in February. These startups will need legal guidance through investment and acquisitions. In response to this 'AI boom', worldwide regulation is evolving. The EU's AI Act is now law and California's governor is considering the state's divisive AI bill. Norton Rose Fulbright is well-positioned to advise businesses in this area, having formalised its AI practice in the USA this year with Chuck Hollis as practice group head. This position could be strengthened by formalising AI practice groups in jurisdictions like the UK or China, which follow the US in global AI investment. Norton Rose Fulbright's multijurisdictional expertise will be vital as businesses fear the difficulties of navigating a "patchwork" of AI global regulations. Another developing field in the technology sector is space and satellites. I chaired Legal Cheek's 'Space and the law' event and was struck by the growth of commercial space projects. With expertise in aviation and telecommunications law, as well as an international approach which aligns with the inherently international nature of space, Norton Rose Fulbright is exceptionally well placed to develop its offering here. The firm released a guide to the sector at the end of 2023; further investment into this area could really boost Norton Rose Fulbright's technology sector.
