## Why have you chosen to apply to HFW and how do you think the firm differs from its competitors? (350 words) HFW's international outreach, particularly in Latin America, is a real selling point for me. As a Spanish speaker with a degree in politics, I have become increasingly fascinated by the political and business climate in the continent. Given the complex legal landscape, as well as the fact that insurance policies are written in English, Spanish and Portuguese, HFW's relationships with firms across the continent (such as with Maciel, Norman & Asociados in Argentina) are vital in offering seamless advice on high value claims. Chambers' ranking of HFW in Band 1 for insurance in Latin America illustrates the firm's market-leading expertise in the region. During the recent AllAboutLaw webinar, 'Life in the Corporate and Commercial Group at HFW', I asked Associate x about the firm's dealings in Latin America on a transactional level. I was captivated by x's comment that although HFW's dealings in Latin America are mainly disputes related, she has worked on multiple projects in the region and has a current client in Chile, reinforcing how international work permeates all of the firm's core sectors. X also alluded to the range of opportunities that speaking foreign languages can bring you at the firm, which has reinforced my desire to pursue a career at HFW. As someone who loved volunteering and interning in Madrid, the prospect of working with the firm's overseas stakeholders and clients as a secondee or otherwise is a hugely exciting prospect. I am also drawn to HFW by the firm's lean trainee intake which allows for access to the firm's senior lawyers. As someone who has the long-term goal of becoming a partner at an international firm, I found observing partners' dealings and learning osmotically while working as an intern in Spain highly motivating. As then Trainee x explained to me at last year's Legal Cheek Virtual Law Fair, the small intake makes for high levels of industry exposure compared with the firm's competitors. As someone sociable and gregarious, this aspect also appeals as it makes for a collaborative environment with fellow trainees, rather than one that encourages trainees to compete with one another.
