If successful in securing a training contract after the Summer Internship, what do you anticipate will be the key challenges of the International Training Programme for you? How would you approach these challenges, and what resources or support do you believe you would need to overcome them? (Max 200 words/1,500 Characters) I anticipate that there will be two key challenges of the International Training Programme, the first of which is time management. Engaging in challenging work such as high-profile cross-border transactions across the Programme will be very time-consuming, especially for trainees looking to develop their knowledge and skills in unfamiliar areas. I would approach this by prioritising tasks effectively, initially drawing upon advice from a supervisor to understand which tasks need to be completed as a priority and then considering this advice when completing work in the future. I would also communicate with fellow trainees to build a support network and ensure that we are all in this together. Secondly, another key challenge will be adjusting to different individuals both within and outside of the firm. Being exposed to four practice areas, potential secondment opportunities, and meeting various clients as part of the Training Programme will require versatility from trainees to adjust to these environments. To approach this I would continue to develop my communication and active listening skills to understand acceptable ways to communicate in these different environments and adjust accordingly. I would also make efforts to build positive working relationships with colleagues and clients alike.
