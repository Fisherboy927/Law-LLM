Describe the personal life experiences that have given you the unique perspective you would bring as an insight scheme participant at Fieldfisher, and how this perspective will support your success. (800 characters) During year 12 I took part in a project with BP and the Engineering Development Trust called GO4SET. This was a 10-week project over the course of which I was tasked with working with some fellow students to identify an instance in which robotics could be implemented at schools. My group and I designed and modelled a chair-stacking robot. The Diversity Access Scheme will give me real-life insight into trainee-level work. Whilst more proficient in the humanities, my participation in GO4SET demonstrates my willingness to go outside my comfort zone and disciplines that are familiar. I will apply this same perspective to learning during the scheme, and I imagine a full-service law firm like Fieldfisher will appreciate this generalist and open-minded approach to learning.
