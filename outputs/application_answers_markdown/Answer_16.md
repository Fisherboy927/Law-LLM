Please provide details of your main extracurricular interests and achievements. How have these helped you to develop the skills that you will need as a commercial solicitor? (350 words) I have attended various law-related careers events, insight days and workshops to gain a solid understanding of commercial law. I recently completed the Winter Vacation Scheme where I gained profound insight into disputes, restructuring, legal tech and listed law firms, supplementing my knowledge with case study exercises. Through these vacation schemes and events, I have gained a profound understanding of a wide range of practices and commercial law topics. I enhanced my skillset working through case studies and was able to network with lawyers and aspiring lawyers. I attended Baker McKenzie's Open Day in 2023 where I collaborated with six aspiring lawyers on a group project which received positive feedback from associates. I understood the benefit of collaborating to combine strengths and produce greater results through synergistic relationships. This experience affirmed how much I enjoy working with others in team environments to understand a business, explore the problems it faces and develop solutions to overcome them. I have a robust sports background, recently participating in a five-kilometre run to support Cancer Research UK and becoming the lead tumbler in my cheerleading team. In 2021, I dedicated a month to learning how to surf in Mexico. I was part of the Oxford Athletics Squad and the gospel choir. I have also represented Newham in the London Youth Games Cross Country. These experiences have instilled in me the values of teamwork, commitment, and discipline - skills crucial to the role of a lawyer. I was an Access & Outreach Officer in the Oxford African & Caribbean Society from 2021 to 2022 where I actively contributed to fostering diversity and inclusion. I enjoyed leading discussion panels and workshops about promoting diversity in institutions and the workplace.
