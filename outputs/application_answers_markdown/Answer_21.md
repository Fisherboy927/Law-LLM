At DLA Piper, we believe great business can make a better world. We believe in being collaborative, bold, exceptional, and supportive. We are keen to hear what inspired your application. Why are you interested in starting a legal career at DLA Piper? (Max 200 words/1,500 Characters) DLA Piper’s strong regional presence, expertise in employment law, and commendable pro bono work drew me to the firm. Having spoken to various trainees, such as Ahmed Syed, at the firm from different offices at Bright Network’s Future Lawyers event it was clear that through the firm’s One UK approach, DLA offers similar experiences and opportunities across all of its offices. This appeals to me as someone with a strong connection to the north who wants to build a career there. Additionally, DLA’s experience in employment law, with a tier one legal 500 ranking for Leeds and West Yorkshire is attractive to me as someone interested in this area. DLA’s work on the headline Uber case, advising the company on the outcome of the classification of its drivers as workers intrigued me and illustrated the calibre of work the firm is involved with. Therefore, my aspirations to conduct work of high commercial importance in the north drew me to the firm. On top of this, the firm’s work as a partner of the charity KIND UK providing advice to undocumented children aligns with my values and commitment to social justice, further attracting me to a legal career at DLA Piper.
