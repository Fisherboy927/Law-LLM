1. When have you worked towards an ambitious goal? As part of your answer, please describe how you tackled this goal, what set backs you faced and what energised you about the situation. (500 words) One vivid example of working towards an ambitious goal was when I set out to develop my own adventure video game from scratch. Captivated by storytelling and the immersive worlds of games since childhood, I was inspired to create a game that offered not just entertainment but a deep, emotionally engaging experience through intricate character development and dynamic narratives. Embarking on this project without formal training in game or software design was a significant undertaking. Driven by the desire to bring my vision to life, I recognised it would require me to self-learn a multitude of complex skills. The game I envisioned featured a richly detailed world with numerous interactive characters, each influencing the storyline in meaningful ways based on player choices. Faced with the challenge of mastering new programming languages, understanding game development frameworks, and learning graphic design independently, I adopted a structured approach to self-learning. Immersing myself in online tutorials, webinars, and developer forums, I broke down the development process into manageable steps with clear milestones. This methodical approach allowed me to build a solid foundation in programming and game design principles. A significant setback occurred when I encountered performance issues during testing phases involving complex character interactions. The frame rate dropped noticeably, causing lag and detracting from the player experience. Improving the game's performance without compromising its rich content was critical. To overcome this hurdle, I delved into optimisation techniques such as object pooling to manage resources efficiently, level-of-detail adjustments to reduce processing loads, and refining code to eliminate unnecessary calculations. Another challenge was integrating Python scripts to handle advanced computational tasks, despite Unity primarily using C+. This presented compatibility issues like misaligned data types leading to real-time errors. I researched and implemented a middleware solution that standardised data types between Python and Unity, enabling efficient data transfer and processing. This not only resolved the compatibility issues but also significantly enhanced the game's performance. What energised me throughout this journey was witnessing the tangible progress of my creation. Each obstacle overcome and improvement made—whether in performance optimisation or storyline enhancement—fuelled my motivation to tackle the next challenge. Collaborating with other developers in online communities such as Discord, provided valuable insights and fostered a sense of camaraderie, which was especially encouraging during difficult phases. Releasing the demo version was an incredibly rewarding milestone. Receiving positive feedback from players who appreciated the seamless character interactions and engaging gameplay validated the hard work and long hours invested. I managed to increase the frame rate by approximately 30%, significantly enhancing the smoothness of gameplay during complex interactions. This experience highlighted the importance of independent learning, adaptability, and resilience. It honed my technical and problem-solving skills and reinforced my confidence in tackling ambitious goals without formal instruction. I am eager to bring these attributes to Eversheds, confident that my ability to learn independently and think creatively will contribute to delivering practical, tailored solutions for clients.
