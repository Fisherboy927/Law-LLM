If successful in securing a training contract after the Summer Internship, what do you anticipate will be the key challenges of the International Training Programme for you? How would you approach these challenges, and what resources or support do you believe you would need to overcome them? DLA Piper’s ‘one UK’ approach excites me as a junior embarking on their career in law. Not only does this offer unparalleled opportunities to network across the UK, but also demonstrates cohesion on a global stage that has been incredible to experience during my work experience; I believe navigating life as a trainee and associate it all about taking every opportunity available and being a personal and approachable individual.
