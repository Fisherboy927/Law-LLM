## In no more than 250 words, what are your motivations for applying to Clifford Chance? I am motivated to pursue a career at Clifford Chance as I find its Band 1 banking and finance practice appealing. I was intrigued by the firm's advice to Nielsen Holdings on its US $16 billion takeover by Elliot Investment Management, one of the largest deals by value this year. My interest in this practice stems from my work placement at Fieldfisher LLP. Working on a suite of corporate authorisations for financing an M&A deal gave me exposure to the finance sector. In addition, negotiating a sustainable linked loan and learning about green bonds in Clifford Chance's virtual internship further motivated me to apply to the firm. Clifford Chance's corporate strength also interests me. Participating in an M&A case study conducted by partner Adrian Levy at Queen Mary University helped me learn more about each stage of an M&A transaction. It further piqued my interest in this sector. The firm has 33 Tier 1 rankings in Legal 500 UK 2023, more than any other London firm. I find this appealing as it will allow me to have a well-rounded training programme in leading practice areas of my choice. Lastly, there are 56.3% female lawyers in Clifford Chance's London Office. This is a testament to the firm's inclusive environment. It gives me confidence that I would be valued at the firm. As a woman of colour belonging to an ethnic minority, the firm's commitment towards diversity and inclusion is important to me.
