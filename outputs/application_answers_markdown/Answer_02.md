## Why are you interested in pursuing a career at Herbert Smith Freehills? (300 words) Herbert Smith Freehills' (HSF) dispute resolution expertise primarily attracts me. I explored this during HSF's Global Litigation Virtual internship, and when shadowing my HSF BASS (Black Aspiring Solicitor Scheme) mentor, [redacted], in International Arbitration. The latter included proofreading a witness interview brief for a billion-dollar arbitration and drafting a counter-offer letter for settlement discussions in a pro bono litigation matter. I've thoroughly enjoyed experiencing first-hand the complex problem-solving involved in cross-jurisdictional commercial and employment dispute resolution, making HSF's guaranteed disputes seat, and various secondment opportunities, particularly appealing. Secondly, HSF’s sincere dedication to career progression highly appeals to me. This is demonstrated by the firm’s consistently high retention rates, even in senior positions, and the innovative HSF Deal framework. I have also realised this through BASS, wherein [redacted] (Associate, Finance) reflected on her supervisor’s genuine investment in giving her opportunities to develop her competencies and explore her interests as a trainee. Through BASS, I’ve recognised the importance of tailored career support, which has been pivotal in building my commercial and legal skills. Knowing that HSF fosters a culture where senior colleagues actively support junior lawyers reinforces my confidence that I, too, could benefit from HSF’s unparalleled opportunities for both personal and professional growth. HSF's commitment to diversity also attracts me. HSF's robust affinity groups, including the Black Employee Network (BEN), and close collaboration with Rare Recruitment and Aspiring Solicitors – organisations which have greatly supported me in developing commercial awareness and building key legal skills through providing mentorship and coaching – have led to HSF's ranking as UK's 5th most ethnically diverse law firm. I find this inclusive environment essential, both, as [redacted] (Senior Associate, Finance) recognised, for attracting and building relationships with international clientele, and because, as a member of multiple underrepresented groups, I desire a workplace that celebrates my unique background.
