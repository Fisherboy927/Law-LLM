## Why do you want to be a solicitor at an international law firm? (350 words) From both my time as an International Relations student and as a legal intern for an international firm in Madrid, I have become increasingly interested in international matters. This fascination led me to voluntarily contribute to the blog of sports lawyer x in 2022. I wrote my article on the sanctions imposed on the Russian Football Union by UEFA and FIFA and the ensuing dispute between the parties in light of Russia's invasion of Ukraine. Given that the blog was intended to inform her layperson clients, I tailored the wording of my article to ensure that it was accessible but informative. I found the process of conducting independent legal research from a variety of sources and drafting my findings for a target audience highly intellectually stimulating. The experience also demonstrated to me how law is inextricably linked to politics and business. The insight I obtained into the international and interconnected nature of commercial law while volunteering undoubtedly piqued my interest and influenced my decision to pursue an internship within the in-house legal department of an international data management company. While working there, I was regularly tasked with conducting contract review. On one occasion, I was tasked with reviewing the terms of an invitation to tender from a US state pension fund. While conducting the analysis, I discovered that the potential client was looking for various contractual assurances such as a guarantee that the firm would not boycott any oil companies in the future. With the company's ESG goals in mind, this provision stood out to me as a potentially onerous term as I believed that acquiescence could lead to becoming contractually pigeonholed in the future. As such, I communicated my thoughts to the General Counsel who was then able to communicate her response to the sales team. I thoroughly enjoyed the investigative nature of the task as well as the process of balancing business and ethical considerations. This experience cemented my desire to pursue a career as a solicitor at a firm like HFW with extensive international clients and offices as well as a compulsory international secondment opportunity.
