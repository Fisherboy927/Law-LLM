What has motivated you to pursue a career in commercial law and more specifically, why have you applied to Ashurst? (300 words) I am keen on pursuing a career in commercial law, particularly because of its transactional nature and its close ties to both politics and business. The challenge of structuring deals, navigating regulatory frameworks, and ensuring that transactions align with legal, political, and business objectives is something that I find particularly enthralling. During a 4-week internship at a top corporate law firm in [Redacted], I gained valuable insights into the political dimensions influencing the regulatory environment in the mining sector—a crucial component of the [Redacted] economy. My role involved conducting legal and political analyses for a significant mining-related transaction initiated by a foreign investor. This experience underscored the importance of political due diligence in understanding how government actions and political stability can influence the legal regulatory framework within a particular jurisdiction. As a result, exposure to such aspects will give me the opportunity to develop legally sound and politically informed solutions for global business challenges. Therefore, I am particularly drawn to Ashurst's extensive international network, especially within the APAC region, which was notably strengthened by the firm's merger with Blake Dawson in 2012. Ashurst's strategic partnerships with firms in Seoul, Shanghai, Singapore, and other key Asian markets have significantly bolstered its energy and infrastructure practice across the region. These alliances not only capitalise on the region's substantial contribution to global GDP but also prioritise renewable energy and decarbonisation investments. My studies in international economic law have deepened my interest in this area, particularly in understanding how ESG factors are increasingly being integrated into financial decision-making processes and how low-carbon technologies are reshaping global markets. Ashurst offers an unparalleled opportunity for trainees to engage in cross-border transactions that align with these critical global shifts while contributing to the firm's commitment to supporting net-zero targets.
