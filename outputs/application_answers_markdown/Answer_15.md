## Why do you want to be a solicitor at an international law firm? (350 words) I thoroughly enjoy the varied day-to-day tasks of a global commercial lawyer and I am motivated by the prospect of being involved in multi-jurisdictional cases and deals. During my 2022 internship with Trowers & Hamlins in Dubai, I worked closely with a private wealth partner to advise an Emirati family on business restructuring. I have a problem-solving mentality and enjoyed strategizing to successfully implement corporate governance around the business. This experience gave me first-hand exposure to the legal sector, business advisory and English law in the Middle East. I would greatly benefit from the learning opportunities that having exposure to high-calibre international work at HFW would provide. Having pursued career opportunities in several countries such as England, Spain, UAE, and Mexico I deeply value learning more about diverse cultures and perspectives. HFW's internationalism is at the core of its strategy. With a global platform which spans 20 locations, the firm has effective and impressive cross-border capabilities. I am excited to develop my skills and global outlook when working on impactful, multijurisdictional projects at the firm. My strengths align with the skillsets required of international lawyers. I can read, write, and analyse pieces of text in multiple languages including English, Portuguese, Spanish and Creole, which has developed me into an excellent analyst and communicator with profound comprehension skills. As a former Business Development Manager, I effectively managed relationships with English, Spanish and Portuguese clients. My frequent international travel to network with transport leaders reinforced my ability to take initiative and work seamlessly across borders. I am eager to contribute my language skills to multijurisdictional projects. I was praised for my empathetic communication and ability to stimulate honest conversations and solution-oriented approaches. These skills will prove invaluable when advising HFW's clients. I was awarded the Crankstart scholarship in 2018 for my academic excellence despite socio-economic obstacles.
