## OCR Text

Describe the personal event or experience that, more than anything else, made you realise your desire to work in a law firm and specifically at Fieldfisher. (800 characters) ACCESS was a scheme run by Clifford Chance to help disadvantaged sixth formers break into commercial law. An application made in curiosity gave me the chance to argue before a part-time judge, and present an elevator pitch before three partners. These experiences were wholly unique to me at the time given the background I was from, and ignited my desire to work in a law firm. What made me realise I want to work at Fieldfisher is my current study of Intellectual Property law at university. I see the value in pursuing more niche practice to get engaged in more under-explored and thus cutting edge work. So Fieldfisher's Band 1 ranking in Clinical Negligence, and offering of services like Immigration and IP, sets the firm out from city competitors that have neglected these niches to me.
