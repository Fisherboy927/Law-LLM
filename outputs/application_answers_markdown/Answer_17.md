## Why have you chosen to apply to HFW and how do you think the firm differs from its competitors? (350 words) I am drawn to HFW's specialist approach to supporting clients. HFW is a leading UK firm in construction law and has advised on various trailblazing rail construction projects, such as the £750 million European rail electrification project and metro schemes across the globe. This sector focus aligns with my professional pursuits and my background in the rail industry. During my legal internship in the UAE, I gained practical experience in construction dispute resolution by preparing a briefing note for a $6.7 billion polymer construction project dispute in Oman. I enjoyed learning about EPC packages and the complexities that arise in infrastructure projects. Unlike their competitors, HFW lawyers have incredibly detailed knowledge of their client's businesses. The firm's shipping sector niche makes it both unique and highly sought out by high-profile clients such as BP and Tintrade with whom trainees can expect to have some contact. This sentiment was shared by HFW's former graduate recruitment assistant, David Roe at the Oxford Law Fair in 2022. I am excited to work at a firm that will enable me to develop the expertise and specific legal skills required to deliver tailored advice to clients. I am attracted to HFW because of its market-leading dispute resolution and litigation practice. This is exemplified in the firm's 2018 triumph in setting aside a £70 million arbitration appeal against their client, Jiangsu Shagang Group Co Ltd. I appreciate the challenges inherent in contentious matters and I desire to begin my legal career at a firm that is renowned for its success in resolving high-stakes disputes. HFW's training programme has further solidified my interest. As a Future Lawyers Winner for international secondments and one of the only firms to guarantee secondments abroad, HFW is truly ahead of its competitors. The firm's small cohort size of just 15 trainees means that it has the resources to truly invest in its trainees, providing them with high-quality work and early responsibility.
