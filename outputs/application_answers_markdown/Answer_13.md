### Can you provide an example of a recent set back you have had, and how you have overcome this. (300 words) As a Board Member of one of the largest charitable youth organisations in [Redacted], I led a maternity drive to support impoverished women in rural areas. This drive focused on providing healthcare materials to women during pregnancy, childbirth, and the post-natal period. The project was ambitious, and along the way, we hit serious setbacks that tested my resolve and commitment. Just weeks before the drive, several key volunteers pulled out due to fast-approaching exam dates. Additionally, we had not yet raised sufficient funds, leading some team members to propose scaling back the project. It was a challenging moment—our resources were limited, and the success of the initiative was at risk. However, I was determined to see the project through as I believed in its importance and was confident that we could overcome these obstacles. I took the lead in rallying the team and spearheading additional fundraising efforts. As part of raising additional funds, we swiftly organised an impromptu raffle, approaching local businesses for last-minute sponsorships to aid with our cause. Their support helped us close the budget gap to meet our goal and further kept the project team's spirits high. Despite the significant challenges we faced, the drive ultimately succeeded, and we surpassed our initial goal by providing essential healthcare toolkits to 200 women. This experience taught me invaluable lessons in adaptability and effective problem-solving. I learned the importance of maintaining composure under pressure and how to pivot strategically when confronted with unforeseen setbacks. By successfully coordinating with a reduced team and securing additional resources on short notice, I sharpened my ability to think critically, manage complex tasks, and lead with precision—skills that are essential in the legal profession. This experience has since shaped my approach to handling challenges in both academic and professional settings.
